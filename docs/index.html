🏅 AgentQuest

Free quest board for Agent ID holders. Claim one free badge, complete one free daily quest. No fees, no payments, no promised returns — the badge is a souvenir.

How it works

  1. Connect your wallet on BNB Chain (chainId 56).
  2. The contract checks on-chain that your wallet holds an Agent ID.
  3. Claim one free badge (1 per wallet, forever).
  4. Complete one free quest per day — a short note about what you or your agent built.

The contract never holds funds and has no payable functions. The owner's only power is updating the badge artwork URI.
